Multiplayer Shooty Game

Originally, this was going to be a tf2 inspired game with classes and matches, but the idea has changed a ton since then. Every time I wanted to work on a new project, like a tank or airplane sim, I just tried a way to merge it into this. Now the goal is a blend between ravenfield and dcs. I want the freedom and detail that dcs has to offer with the customizability of ravenfield. I don't think it'll get finished.
I've been inspired by countless games over the years and I'd like to combine all the best parts into something that I would have fun with. I don't want to have the complexity of DCS in order to play with friends who don't want to just learn a plane, but I don't want it to be as simple as project reality. A VTOL VR or Derail Valley experience in terms of complexity seems perfect.
