Multiplayer Shooty Game

Originally, this was going to be a tf2 inspired game with classes and matches, but the idea has changed a ton since then. Every time I wanted to work on a new project, like a tank or airplane sim, I just tried a way to merge it into this. The goal eventually became a blend between ravenfield and dcs. I wanted the freedom and detail that dcs has to offer with the customizability of ravenfield.
I've been inspired by countless games over the years and I wanted to combine all the best parts into something. I didn't want the complexity of DCS so I could play with friends who don't want to learn an actual airplane, but I didn't want it to be as simple as project reality or ravenfield. The goal was a VTOL VR or Derail Valley type experience, but for a milsim.
This project was too ambitious. I knew it would be time intensive from the beginning, but with shifting interests (I don't want to sit at a computer all day now) I've abandoned this one. I will leave this up for posterity or something.
