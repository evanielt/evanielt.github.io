who this is for

This website is mostly for myself, for me see where I've been and what I've done. But I also can show it to others when it comes up. I saw this video about photography and creation, and I thought it was pretty interesting.

I want to make things; but why do I want to make them? Who is it for? It's likely both for myself and for those around me. I'm not interested in a large audience, but a community of those I can show it to is nice.

Methodology (is this the right word)

I've tried to keep this website simple and lightweight. I want it to be stylish and clean, but also fun and information heavy. For much of the website (especially the blogs), I've taken inspiration from Gorkem's webspace, a very cool personal website. It feels like the "glory days" of the web. I have intentionally left off bloat like related articles in order to keep this a simple, usable website for me to do what I want.
