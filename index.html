I'm working on it

A big inspiration for this site has been Japanese car ads from the 80's. What I've I noticed:

  • They don't have borders around things
  • The photographs have sharp corners, no border radius
  • They don't usually have drop shadow, but I need to keep the drop shadow to add some separation between elements
  • Lots of dithering and grain
  • Honda in particular made use of geometric shapes

I'm happy with it so far, but I want to lean into the 80's graphic design even more. I know I need more dithering and gradients (especially on the tiles) and I should probably add some geometric icons or ribbons. I gotta remove the border radius but I couldn't figure out how without making it look clunky. My background gradient has gotta be better too.
